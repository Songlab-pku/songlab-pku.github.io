## About the Lab

We study the neural circuit designing principles to understand basic animal behaviors such as sensorimotor integration, learning and memory. Our research uses laboratory mouse and spiders as the model animals. With the state-of-the-art technologies ranging from light, X-ray to electron microscopy, we resolve neural circuit microstructures at synaptic resolutions.

## About Me
