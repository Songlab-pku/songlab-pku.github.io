## Background

Assistant Professor & Principal Investigator at the National Biomedical Imaging Center, Future Technology College, Peking University.

Our lab uses laboratory mouse and spiders as model animals. With state-of-the-art technologies ranging from light, X-ray to electron microscopy, we resolve neural circuit microstructures at synaptic resolutions.
